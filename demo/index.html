Why the baseline can have higher accuracy

The majority baseline predicts that everybody stays. It gets many records correct because most people stayed, but detects zero leavers. Recall and precision show this tradeoff. The selected model's false alarms must also be reported.

What this project establishes

A reproducible comparison on an educational HR dataset. It does not establish causes of resignation, a prediction time horizon, fairness, or real-world readiness. Model scores are not calibrated personal resignation probabilities.
